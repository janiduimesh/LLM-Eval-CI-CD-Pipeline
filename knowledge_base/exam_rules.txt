================================================================================
UNIVERSITY EXAMINATION RULES & POLICIES
================================================================================

1. EXAM SCHEDULING
   - Final examinations are scheduled by the Registrar's Office and published
     at least 4 weeks before the examination period begins.
   - Midterm examinations are scheduled by individual instructors and must be
     announced at least 2 weeks in advance.
   - No exams may be scheduled during the last week of regular classes (known
     as the "dead week") except for lab practicals.
   - Students with 3 or more exams on the same day may request rescheduling
     of one exam through the Registrar's Office.

2. EXAM CONDUCT
   - Students must arrive at least 15 minutes before the scheduled exam time.
   - Late arrivals up to 30 minutes may be admitted but will not receive extra
     time. Students arriving more than 30 minutes late will not be admitted.
   - Students must present a valid university ID card to be admitted to the
     examination hall.
   - All personal belongings (bags, phones, smartwatches) must be stored in
     the designated area at the front of the examination hall.

3. PERMITTED MATERIALS
   - Closed-book exams: No materials allowed except writing instruments.
   - Open-book exams: Only physical textbooks and printed notes are allowed
     unless the instructor specifies otherwise. Electronic devices are
     prohibited.
   - Calculator-permitted exams: Only non-programmable, non-graphing
     calculators are allowed. The approved models list is published on the
     university website.
   - Formula sheets: If permitted, they must be on a single A4 page,
     handwritten by the student.

4. ACADEMIC DISHONESTY DURING EXAMS
   - Any form of cheating during exams results in immediate expulsion from
     the examination hall.
   - The student receives a zero for the exam and is reported to the Academic
     Integrity Board.
   - Prohibited behaviors include: communicating with other students, using
     unauthorized materials, copying from another student's paper, and
     using electronic devices.
   - Proctors have the authority to confiscate suspicious materials and
     reassign seating during the exam.

5. MAKE-UP EXAMINATIONS
   - Make-up exams are only granted for documented medical emergencies,
     bereavement (immediate family), or university-sanctioned conflicts.
   - Students must submit a make-up exam request within 48 hours of the
     missed exam along with supporting documentation.
   - Make-up exams are typically scheduled within 2 weeks of the original
     exam date.
   - The format of the make-up exam may differ from the original exam at
     the instructor's discretion.

6. GRADE APPEALS
   - Students may appeal their exam grade within 10 business days of grade
     publication.
   - Appeals must be submitted in writing to the Department Head with a
     detailed explanation of the grounds for appeal.
   - Valid grounds include: arithmetic errors in grading, failure to grade
     a section, or evidence of bias.
   - The appeal is reviewed by a committee of 3 faculty members (excluding
     the original instructor). The committee's decision is final.
   - The re-evaluated grade may be higher, lower, or the same as the
     original grade.

7. EXAM DURATION AND FORMAT
   - Standard final exams are 3 hours in duration.
   - Midterm exams are typically 1.5 to 2 hours in duration.
   - Exam formats may include: multiple choice, short answer, essay,
     problem-solving, or a combination thereof.
   - The exam format and topic coverage must be communicated to students
     at least 1 week before the exam.

8. PROCTORING
   - All exams with more than 50 students require a minimum of 2 proctors.
   - Proctors must complete the university's Proctor Training Module before
     supervising exams.
   - Online proctored exams use the university-approved proctoring software
     and require a webcam and stable internet connection.
   - Any technical issues during online proctored exams must be reported
     immediately to the exam support hotline.
