================================================================================
UNIVERSITY COURSE REGISTRATION RULES & POLICIES
================================================================================

1. REGISTRATION PERIODS
   - Priority registration opens 6 weeks before the start of each semester.
     Priority is determined by class standing: seniors register first, followed
     by juniors, sophomores, and freshmen.
   - Open registration begins 4 weeks before the semester and is available to
     all students regardless of class standing.
   - Late registration is permitted during the first week of classes with a
     late registration fee of $50.
   - Registration closes at the end of the first week of classes.

2. COURSE PREREQUISITES
   - Students must complete all prerequisite courses with a minimum grade of
     C (2.0 GPA equivalent) before enrolling in advanced courses.
   - Prerequisite overrides may be granted by the department chair with
     documented justification (e.g., transfer credits, professional experience).
   - Co-requisite courses must be taken simultaneously in the same semester.
   - The registration system automatically enforces prerequisite requirements
     and will block enrollment if prerequisites are not met.

3. ADD/DROP POLICY
   - Students may add courses during the first week of classes without penalty.
   - Students may drop courses without academic record during the first 2 weeks
     of classes. Dropped courses will not appear on the transcript.
   - Withdrawal after the 2-week period but before the 10th week results in a
     "W" (Withdrawal) grade on the transcript. This does not affect GPA.
   - Withdrawal after the 10th week is not permitted except in cases of
     documented medical emergency or extreme personal hardship.

4. CREDIT HOUR LIMITS
   - Full-time students must register for a minimum of 12 credit hours and a
     maximum of 18 credit hours per semester.
   - Students with a cumulative GPA of 3.5 or higher may request permission
     to register for up to 21 credit hours (overload).
   - Overload requests must be approved by the academic advisor and the
     Dean of the student's college.
   - Part-time students may register for 1 to 11 credit hours per semester.
   - Summer session limits are 6 credit hours per summer term (12 total for
     both summer terms).

5. WAITLIST POLICY
   - If a course section is full, students may join the waitlist through the
     registration system.
   - Waitlist positions are processed on a first-come, first-served basis.
   - Students are automatically enrolled when a seat becomes available and are
     notified via their university email.
   - Students have 24 hours to confirm their enrollment after being moved
     from the waitlist. Failure to confirm results in removal from the course
     and the next waitlisted student is enrolled.
   - The maximum waitlist size is 10 students per course section.

6. COURSE SECTION CHANGES
   - Students may switch between sections of the same course during the
     add/drop period without penalty.
   - Section changes after the add/drop period require instructor approval
     from both the original and new section instructors.
   - Section changes are subject to seat availability.

7. ACADEMIC STANDING REQUIREMENTS
   - Students on academic probation (cumulative GPA below 2.0) are limited
     to 14 credit hours per semester.
   - Students on academic probation must meet with their academic advisor
     before registration is enabled.
   - Two consecutive semesters on academic probation may result in academic
     dismissal.

8. CROSS-REGISTRATION
   - Students may cross-register for courses at partner institutions through
     the consortium agreement.
   - Cross-registration is limited to 2 courses per semester and requires
     approval from both institutions.
   - Credits earned through cross-registration are transferred as pass/fail
     unless otherwise specified.
   - Cross-registration forms must be submitted at least 3 weeks before the
     start of the semester.
