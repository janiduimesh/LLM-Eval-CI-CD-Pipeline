================================================================================
UNIVERSITY PAYMENT & FINANCIAL RULES & POLICIES
================================================================================

1. TUITION AND FEES
   - Tuition is charged on a per-credit-hour basis. The current rate is $500
     per credit hour for undergraduate students and $750 per credit hour for
     graduate students.
   - Mandatory fees include: Student Activity Fee ($150/semester), Technology
     Fee ($200/semester), Health Services Fee ($100/semester), and Library
     Fee ($75/semester).
   - Lab fees of $50-$200 per course apply to courses with laboratory
     components. The specific fee is listed in the course catalog.
   - Tuition rates are reviewed annually and may increase by up to 5% per
     academic year.

2. PAYMENT DEADLINES
   - Full payment or enrollment in a payment plan is required by the tuition
     deadline, which is 2 weeks before the start of each semester.
   - Students who fail to pay by the deadline may have their registration
     cancelled.
   - A confirmation of financial responsibility must be completed online
     before registration is finalized.

3. PAYMENT METHODS
   - Accepted payment methods include: bank transfer, credit card (Visa,
     MasterCard, American Express), debit card, certified check, and
     approved third-party payments (employer, embassy, sponsor).
   - Cash payments are accepted only at the Bursar's Office during business
     hours (Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 4 PM).
   - Online payments can be made through the Student Financial Portal 24/7.
   - International wire transfers must include the student's university ID
     number as a reference. Processing takes 5-7 business days.

4. INSTALLMENT PAYMENT PLAN
   - Students may enroll in a semester installment plan that divides tuition
     into 4 equal monthly payments.
   - A non-refundable enrollment fee of $50 is required to set up the
     installment plan.
   - The first installment is due on the tuition deadline. Subsequent
     installments are due on the 1st of each following month.
   - Missing an installment payment results in a $25 late fee per occurrence.
   - Two missed installment payments will result in removal from the plan
     and the full remaining balance becomes due immediately.

5. LATE PAYMENT FEES
   - A late payment fee of $100 is assessed if tuition is not paid by the
     deadline and the student is not enrolled in a payment plan.
   - An additional $25 per week is charged for each week the balance remains
     unpaid, up to a maximum of $200 in additional late fees.
   - Students with outstanding balances exceeding 60 days will have a
     financial hold placed on their account, preventing registration for
     future semesters, transcript requests, and diploma release.

6. REFUND POLICY
   - Students who withdraw from courses are eligible for tuition refunds
     based on the following schedule:
     * Before the start of classes: 100% refund
     * During the first week: 90% refund
     * During the second week: 75% refund
     * During the third week: 50% refund
     * During the fourth week: 25% refund
     * After the fourth week: No refund
   - Mandatory fees are non-refundable after the start of classes.
   - Refunds are processed within 2-4 weeks and returned via the original
     payment method.
   - Students who are dismissed for academic or disciplinary reasons are
     not eligible for refunds.

7. FINANCIAL AID AND SCHOLARSHIPS
   - Financial aid packages are determined by the Financial Aid Office based
     on the FAFSA application and institutional aid applications.
   - The FAFSA deadline for priority consideration is March 1st for the
     following academic year.
   - Merit-based scholarships require a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 for
     renewal. GPA is reviewed at the end of each academic year.
   - Need-based grants do not require repayment. Student loans must be
     repaid according to federal and institutional loan terms.
   - Work-study positions are available for eligible students and provide
     hourly compensation (not applied directly to tuition).
   - Scholarship recipients must maintain full-time enrollment (12+ credit
     hours) to retain their scholarship.

8. THIRD-PARTY BILLING
   - Students sponsored by employers, government agencies, or embassies
     must submit an official authorization letter or purchase order to the
     Bursar's Office before the tuition deadline.
   - The authorization must specify the amount covered and any conditions.
   - The student is responsible for any balance not covered by the third
     party.
   - Third-party payments not received within 60 days of the semester start
     become the student's responsibility.
