================================================================================
UNIVERSITY ASSIGNMENT RULES & POLICIES
================================================================================

1. SUBMISSION DEADLINES
   - All assignments must be submitted by 11:59 PM on the due date specified in
     the course syllabus.
   - The official submission platform is the university's Learning Management
     System (LMS). Email submissions are not accepted unless explicitly
     authorized by the instructor.
   - Students are responsible for verifying that their submission was uploaded
     correctly. A confirmation receipt is generated upon successful submission.

2. LATE SUBMISSION POLICY
   - Assignments submitted after the deadline will incur a penalty of 10% per
     day, up to a maximum of 3 days late.
   - After 3 days past the deadline, the assignment will receive a score of zero.
   - Technical issues (e.g., internet outage) must be reported to the IT Help
     Desk within 2 hours of the deadline to qualify for an exception.
   - Documentation (screenshots, ISP confirmation) is required for technical
     exception requests.

3. EXTENSIONS
   - Extension requests must be submitted at least 48 hours before the deadline.
   - Valid reasons for extensions include: documented medical emergencies,
     bereavement, university-sanctioned events, and religious observances.
   - Extensions are granted at the discretion of the instructor and may not
     exceed 7 calendar days beyond the original deadline.
   - Students may request a maximum of 2 extensions per course per semester.

4. PLAGIARISM AND ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
   - All submitted work must be original. Plagiarism includes copying from
     other students, online sources, or AI-generated content without proper
     attribution.
   - Assignments are processed through plagiarism detection software. A
     similarity score above 25% will trigger a review.
   - First offense: The student receives a zero on the assignment and a formal
     warning letter.
   - Second offense: The student fails the course and is referred to the
     Academic Integrity Board.
   - Third offense: The student faces suspension or expulsion from the
     university.

5. GROUP ASSIGNMENTS
   - Group assignments must include a contribution statement signed by all
     members.
   - Each group member must contribute a minimum of 20% to the project.
   - Disputes regarding contribution must be raised with the instructor before
     the submission deadline.
   - Free-riding (contributing less than 10%) may result in individual grading
     at a reduced scale.

6. GRADING RUBRIC
   - All assignments are graded on a standardized rubric provided at the time
     of assignment distribution.
   - Rubric categories typically include: Content Accuracy (40%), Critical
     Analysis (25%), Presentation & Formatting (20%), and References (15%).
   - Instructors must return graded assignments within 14 business days of
     the submission deadline.

7. RESUBMISSION POLICY
   - Resubmission is permitted only for assignments that scored below 50%,
     and only if the student attended all related tutorials.
   - Resubmitted assignments are capped at a maximum grade of 60%.
   - Only one resubmission attempt is allowed per assignment.
   - Resubmissions must be completed within 7 days of receiving the graded
     feedback.

8. SPECIAL ACCOMMODATIONS
   - Students with documented disabilities may receive extended deadlines
     (typically 25% additional time) through the Disability Support Office.
   - Accommodation letters must be submitted to the instructor within the
     first two weeks of the semester.
   - Alternative assessment formats may be arranged on a case-by-case basis.
